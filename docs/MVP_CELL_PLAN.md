# MVP Cell Plan — Canal Garage Cell

Working name: **Canal Garage Cell**
Target size: 300x300 Project Zomboid tiles (one cell)
Phase: 1 (first playable prototype)

---

## Concept

A dense, gritty block capturing the Sud-Ouest Montreal vibe: industrial on one edge,
residential in the middle, a corner store anchoring street life, and a garage that
doubles as a loot target and navigation landmark. The feel is late-autumn, post-outbreak,
mid-week — the kind of block where nothing dramatic ever happened and now everything has.

---

## Cell contents

### Roads

- **Rue Principale** (main road): runs east-west across the cell, two lanes,
  cracked asphalt, parked cars.
- **Ruelle des Garages** (side street): runs north from Principale, one lane,
  narrows toward the north edge.
- **Service lane / alley**: tight alley behind the row houses, accessible on foot,
  chainlink on one side.

### Buildings

| Building | Notes |
|---|---|
| Row houses (x4) | Two-storey brick, narrow frontage, small yards, chain-link fences |
| Garage / auto shop | Main loot target; roll-up doors, oil stains, a lift, parts shelving |
| Depanneur / corner store | Corner of Principale and Ruelle; lottery tickets, chips, drinks, cash |
| Fenced industrial yard | North edge; metal fence, pallets, propane, possibly a trailer |

### Spawn and navigation

- One spawn point: sidewalk in front of the depanneur.
- Visible landmark: the garage's hand-painted sign facing Rue Principale.
  No real-world logo or brand — fictional name only (e.g., "Garage Bellemare").
  This sign is the cell's recognizable anchor without replicating any real location.

---

## Atmosphere notes

- Graffiti on the alley wall (no real-world tags — invented only).
- A rusted pickup in the industrial yard.
- Overflowing blue bins on alley day.
- One corner lot with a dead garden and lawn furniture.

---

## Out of scope for this cell

- Custom tiles or custom sprites.
- Multiple spawn points.
- Map metadata tiles or minimap integration (Phase 3).
- Any content requiring Build 42-specific assets until B42 tooling is verified.

Install and verify TileZed, WorldEd, and BuildingEd (B41 or B42 as appropriate).
